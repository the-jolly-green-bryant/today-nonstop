Today Nonstop Privacy Policy

Last updated July 28, 2026

Today Nonstop is a single-player interactive story published by Bryant James. You can play without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or other contact information.

Information stored on your device

Your story progress, choices, unlocked memories, and settings are stored locally on your device. This information is used to save and restore your game. It is removed when you erase your game data or uninstall the app, subject to your device's own backup behavior.

Analytics

Today Nonstop may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate game usage, including app starts, story progress, loop counts, milestones, and selected dialogue choices. Advertising storage, ad personalization, and analytics storage are disabled. The app does not use analytics to show ads or build advertising profiles.

Google may process technical information needed to provide analytics, such as device or app information and an abbreviated or anonymized IP address. Analytics is used to improve pacing, reliability, and story design.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number is required.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone data is used.
  • The app contains no advertising or in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Children

Today Nonstop is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

Google Analytics is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's practices change. Questions can be sent to bryant@bryantjames.com.
